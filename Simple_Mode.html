06 / What to Avoid

✕Daily inverse ETFs (SQQQ, 7568, etc.) — they decay in chop and are days-only tools for an active book. Holding one while you "wait and see" just bleeds. Wrong instrument for cash-and-react.
✕Hedging when you hold no book — a hedge protects existing exposure. From cash it's just a paid directional bet that decays.
✕Running bull and bear at once — "covering all bases" from cash mostly means losing a little in every direction.
✕Trading the noise — there are 3 events that matter. Sitting out everything else is a strategy, not a failure.

07 / How & Where — execution quick-reference

Account & approvals

Shares + SGOV need a plain brokerage account. The one put spread needs options Level 2 (defined-risk spreads). You do not need VIX/index-options approval in this simple version.

Order types

Shares/ETFs: limit order. Put spread: a single "put debit spread" ticket at a limit near the mid — never market-order a spread, and never leg in two separate trades.

Where the cash lives

SGOV/BIL or a government MMF inside the trading account — in a margin account a sale frees buying power same day, so you can redeploy instantly.

Sizing discipline

Longs: starter + add on weakness. The put spread: premium = max loss, so size it to a number you'd shrug off. Cash is the rest — and it's a position, not a placeholder.
