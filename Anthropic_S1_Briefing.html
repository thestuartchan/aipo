03 / Risk Factors — what the roadshow will fight over

No public risk section yet (confidential). These are the structural debates already visible. Severity tags are my read.

Valuation compression HIGH
~20× run-rate only holds if revenue keeps roughly doubling. Public markets may mark it below the $965B private mark → below-valuation IPO risk that dents morale, customers, and future raises.
Compute cost & circularity HIGH
~$80B cloud through 2029; ~$1.25B/mo GPU via SpaceX Colossus; ~$30B Azure. Backers are also the vendors — they fund Anthropic and sell it infra. Profit needs revenue to keep outrunning compute inflation.
Competition / commoditization HIGH
OpenAI, Google DeepMind, xAI and open-source (Meta Llama, Mistral) can erode model pricing power in a four-way capability race.
PBC + LTBT governance MED
Mission ("safe AI") sits above shareholder value; the Long-Term Benefit Trust can override investor interests. Public buyers get economics with constrained control.
Run-rate vs realized MED
$47B is an annualized snapshot off a projected quarter, not booked FY revenue (~$10B FY25). Mind the gap between run-rate and realized.
Safety-first leaves revenue on table MED
Held back Claude Mythos Preview (Apr) over cyber-misuse concerns. The discipline is the brand — but bears read it as capped monetization vs. less-restrained rivals.

04 / The Bull / Bear Split

▲ The Case

  • Cheapest multiple of the trio — ~20× vs OpenAI 34–40×, SPCX ~95×
  • Enterprise-led: Claude Code + 300k+ business customers; safety focus wins regulated buyers
  • Approaching profitability — break-even targeted 2028, ~$559M projected Q2'26 op profit (2 yrs ahead of OpenAI)
  • Run-rate (~$47B) reportedly now above OpenAI's (~$25B), driven by Claude Code

▼ The Overhang

  • $80B compute through 2029; ~$1.25B/mo GPU — profit hostage to compute inflation
  • Circular consortium — Amazon/Google/MSFT/Nvidia/Samsung/SK Hynix invest and sell infra
  • 5.3× in 8 months ($183B → $965B) — marks may be ahead of fundamentals
  • PBC/LTBT governance overhang; no audited financials until the public S-1
