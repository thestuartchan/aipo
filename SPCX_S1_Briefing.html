04 / Segment Economics — the bull/bear split

▲ The Bull Engine — Starlink

  • $11.4B FY25 revenue = 61% of total
  • $4.4B operating profit — only segment green on GAAP
  • ~10M subscribers (5M → 10M in early 2026)
  • ARPU −18% to ~$81/mo — growth via cheaper plans = margin watch-point

▼ The Cash Drain — AI / xAI

  • xAI losses >$6B in 2025, $2.5B in Q1'26 alone
  • Q1'26 total net loss −$4.28B in a single quarter
  • Colossus data centre: capital-intensive, GPU-heavy
  • Co. reportedly weighing in-house GPU mfg to cut cost

Launch is profitable but small vs. Starlink; the consolidated GAAP loss is essentially Starlink profit minus AI burn minus heavy non-cash D&A/SBC. The whole valuation narrative rests on Starlink holding margin while AI losses stabilise.

China / state competition MED
