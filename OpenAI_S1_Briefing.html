03 / Risk Factors — what the roadshow will fight over

No public risk section yet (confidential). These are the structural debates already visible. Severity tags are my read.

Cash burn / path to profit HIGH
~$14B operating loss in '26; not profitable until ~2030; HSBC sees ~$207B more funding needed. Most cash-hungry name ever to approach the public market.
Valuation HIGH
>$1T target on ~$25B ARR = ~34–40× revenue while deeply loss-making. Entirely a faith-in-dominance multiple.
Governance structure HIGH
For-profit PBC controlled by a nonprofit Foundation that appoints the board; CEO holds 0% equity. Genuine overhang public investors rarely accept cleanly.
Microsoft co-opetition MED
Largest holder now names OpenAI a competitor; rev-share capped through 2030; OpenAI can now use any cloud. Relationship value is nuanced, not pure tailwind.
Timeline slip MED
CFO reportedly signaled "not ready"; missed internal targets. SEC review runs 60–90d. September could slip to Q1 2027.
Compute concentration MED
~$600B of 5-yr commitments (Oracle ~$300B, AWS ~$38B). Huge fixed obligations against uncertain revenue.
