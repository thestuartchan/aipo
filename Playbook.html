01 / The Core Thesis

The first debut sets the risk appetite for the rest. SpaceX (12 Jun) is the tape's mood-setter — a fixed $135 price on only ~5% float means it almost certainly pops, but the day-2-to-week-2 behaviour is the real signal. A pop that holds emboldens the AI book; a break of issue price within days is the "top-is-in" tell that travels straight to OpenAI and Anthropic.

The AI debuts are the systemic test, not SpaceX. The fragile thing in this market is the web of circular AI financing (vendor-investors funding their own customers). The moment that matters is whether OpenAI prices at or below its last private mark ($852B) in Q4 — a down-mark repudiates private valuations across the board and is the single most likely cascade trigger. Anthropic is the higher-quality, cheaper name (~20× vs OpenAI ~34–40×) and most likely to hold its mark; it's the long side of the quality pair.

Trade the information events, not just direction. The public S-1s (OpenAI ~Aug) are when private fictions meet audited reality. Position into them with defined risk, and let the reaction — not your prior — size the next move. The dashboard's escalation ladder is the governor: GREEN = stay long & keep hedges cheap, AMBER = de-risk & build, RED = defend & get flat on beta.

07 / Risk Register & Escape Plans

◆ Master "get-flat" trigger

If the Dashboard ladder hits RED — i.e. HYG breaking down (credit) AND VIX >30 together, or ≥4 stress gauges bearish — cut beta to the core, let only defined-risk hedges and convexity run, and stop adding new longs. Credit + vol cracking together is the signal that the IPOs have turned from thermometer into accelerant.

Being early on the bear
The melt-up can outlast a naked short. Escape built-in: every bearish line here is a put spread (capped cost) or a time-boxed daily ETF — never a naked short you must defend.
Daily-ETF decay
SQQQ/7568/7347/7515 reset daily and bleed in chop. Escape: hard time-stop measured in days; if the down-move doesn't come fast, you're out — they are not holds, ever.
Proxy slippage
AMZN/GOOGL move on far more than Anthropic; SFTBY is leveraged and gappy. Escape: size for the dilution, treat them as read-throughs, and don't confuse a proxy bet for a pure one.
Liquidity / access
HK inverse ETFs and pre-IPO secondaries are thin and can gap. Escape: keep them small, tactical, and never the core of the hedge — the index put spreads are the backbone.
▲ The reload escape (upside)
The cash reserve is an active position: a strong debut that dips, or a RED capitulation, is when dry powder earns its keep. Plan the buy before the panic, not during.

08 / Expectations — base rates, honestly

Most likely path = chop/rotation (~45–50%), not a clean melt-up or clean cascade. Plan for event-by-event trading, not a single regime bet. SpaceX probably pops (thin float + fixed price) but a pop is not a durable hold — separate the two.

The real systemic test is OpenAI vs its $852B mark in Q4 — that, not SpaceX's debut, is the date the whole hedge book is built around. And the most probable crack trigger isn't an IPO at all: it's a circular-deal name (NVDA/Oracle/CoreWeave) missing or credit spreads widening, with the IPOs as the accelerant. Watch the ladder, keep convexity cheap and carried, keep powder dry, and let the events — not your priors — size each move.
